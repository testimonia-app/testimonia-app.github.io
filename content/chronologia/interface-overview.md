The Chronologia window has three main columns and a toolbar.

1. **Calendars sidebar** — Turn bundled and personal event packs on or off. Use the controls at the bottom to add or reload event packs.
2. **Calendar view** — Browse dates in a month, year, or multi-year view. The content changes when you switch between Grid and Fasti display styles.
3. **Inspector** — Review information about the selected day or event. The **Day** tab shows calendar conversions, astronomical events, feast days, and enabled historical events. The **Event** tab shows event details and editing controls.
4. **Toolbar** — Move backward or forward, jump to a date, choose display options, or search for a historical date or event.

![The complete Chronologia window, with the event-pack sidebar, calendar view, toolbar, and inspector.](/images/chronologia/Full-UI.png)

## Select a day

Click a day in the calendar. Chronologia highlights the date and updates the **Day** tab in the inspector.

The inspector can include:

- The selected date in the active calendar
- Equivalent dates in other calendar systems
- Astronomical events
- Feast days
- Events from enabled event packs

Select an event in the inspector or calendar to open it in the **Event** tab.
