## Move backward or forward

Use the left and right arrow buttons in the toolbar. Each click moves by the unit shown in the current view: one month, one year, or one step in the multi-year view.

## Jump to a date

Enter a date in the field between the arrow buttons, then press Return. Chronologia accepts year and month forms as well as era notation, including examples such as:

- `1270`
- `1270-02`
- `Feb 1270`
- `44 BCE`
- `AD 44`
- Astronomical year `0` or a negative year

The era badge beside the field shows the notation currently in use.

![The Chronologia toolbar above the calendar, including the previous and next buttons and the date field.](/images/chronologia/Full-UI.png)

## Change the display

Open **View Options** in the toolbar to change:

- **Calendar** — Choose the calendar system used by the main view.
- **Polity** — Choose a historical polity when using polity civil or regnal year reckoning.
- **Follow Polity Calendar** — Use the civil calendar historically associated with the selected polity.
- **New Year** — Follow the polity, use the calendar default, or begin the displayed historical year on 1 January, 25 March, 1 September, or 25 December.
- **Week Layout** — Choose a supported week cycle. Available choices depend on the calendar.
- **Zoom** — Switch among Month, Year, and Multi-Year views.
- **Display** — Switch between Grid and Fasti styles.
- **Year Notation** — Use CE/BCE, AD/BC, or astronomical numbering where supported.
- **Roman Day Names in Julian Calendar** — Show or hide Roman day names in the Julian calendar.
